Engineering deliverables & technical reviews

From process models and drawings to equipment specifications, procurement and safety.

Process simulation, HMB & PFD/P&ID

Process simulation and optimization, heat and material balances (HMB), and preparation, verification and approval of process flow diagrams (PFD) and piping and instrumentation diagrams (P&ID).

Pumps, piping & material selection

Equipment and piping sizing: flow, head / ΔP, NPSHa versus NPSHr, absorbed power and minimum flow. Material selection, corrosion allowance, line diameter, schedule, fittings, and hydraulic and thermal losses.

Heat exchangers, furnaces, columns & separators

Process equipment sizing, thermal duty, heat-transfer area, fouling, pressure-drop limits and Aspen EDR reviews. Fired-heater combustion and radiant / convective sections; column internals, separators and hydraulic sizing.

Control valves, PSV / PRV & instruments

Control-valve sizing: Cv, available ΔP, cavitation and flashing. PSV / PRV relief scenarios, discharge flow, set pressure, backpressure and orifice selection. Selection and specification of process instruments; flare hydraulics and depressurization studies.

Equipment datasheets & Bill of Materials

Prepare and review equipment datasheets, instrument specifications, bills of materials (BOM), technical requisitions and supplier offers, including API 610 pump datasheets and technical compliance checks.

HAZOP / HAZID & cause-and-effect matrices

Participate in HAZOP / HAZID reviews, assess deviations and safeguards, prepare cause-and-effect matrices, and support management of change (MOC), technical reviews and safety requirements.
